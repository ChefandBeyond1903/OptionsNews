What do these words mean?
Call
A bet the stock goes UP. You profit if it rises enough before the option expires.
Put
A bet the stock goes DOWN. You profit if it falls enough.
Strike
The price line the option is based on.
Premium / Cost
What you pay for the option. One contract = 100 shares, so a $6 premium costs $600. This is the most you can lose when buying.
Confidence
How many of the signals (trend, momentum, volume, market, news) agree. It is not a probability of winning.
VIX
The market's "fear gauge." Above 25 means big swings and expensive options.
Expiry
The date the option stops existing. Weekly options lose value fast, so don't hold them to expiry.
Signals come from trend, momentum, strength vs. the market, volume, breakouts, news sentiment and overall market conditions, with live option prices when available. This is not financial advice. A bought option can lose 100% of its cost. Only risk money you can afford to lose.
